Privacy policy

Shopping List, by Loye 10 Ventures, LLC. Effective November 27, 2026.

Shopping List is a shared shopping list for households and organizations. This policy explains what the app stores, why, who else handles it, and how to delete it. "We" means Loye 10 Ventures, LLC.

What we collect

  • Your Google sign-in: your name, email address, profile photo and a user ID from Google. We never see your Google password.
  • What you put in your lists: items, notes, quantities, stores, prices, purchases, trips and list settings.
  • Receipts: receipts you log, and photos of receipts if you choose to scan one.
  • People in your lists: the names and email addresses of people you invite, and of people who invite you.
  • Price lookups: a monthly count of lookups for each list, used to keep within the limit.

We don't use advertising, ad tracking or analytics tools, and we don't collect your location.

How we use it

Only to run the app: showing your lists to you and the people you share them with, suggesting items that are due, combining trips, showing spending, reading scanned receipts and looking up store prices when you ask. We don't sell your information or share it for advertising.

Who else handles it

These companies process data for us so the app can work. They aren't allowed to use it for their own purposes.

  • Google Firebase: sign-in and the database where your lists are stored. Firebase App Check uses Google reCAPTCHA to block automated abuse.
  • Google Gemini, through Firebase AI Logic: reads a receipt photo when you scan one, to pull out the items and prices.
  • Netlify: hosts the app and runs the price lookup service.
  • SerpApi: receives the name of an item when you tap Look up prices, to find store prices. It doesn't receive your name or email.

Everyone you add to a list can see that list's items, purchases, receipts and members. That is the point of a shared list, so only invite people you trust with that information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete an item, a list or your account, it is removed from our database right away. Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a limited time before they are overwritten.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, tap your list's name at the top, then Delete my account. Lists that only you belong to are deleted with everything in them. Lists you share are kept for the other members, and you are removed from them. You can also request deletion without the app.

Security

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules allow only the members of a list to read or change it.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Big changes will also be shown in the app.
