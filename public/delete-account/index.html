Or delete it yourself in the app

Sign in, tap your list's name at the top, then tap Delete my account. It happens right away.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in and profile (name, email and photo from Google).
  • Every list that only you belong to, with all its items, purchases, receipts and receipt photos.
  • Invites you sent or received.

What is kept

  • Lists you share with others stay for them. You are removed from them, and if you were the only owner, another member becomes owner.
  • Items and purchases you added to a shared list stay in that list, because they belong to the whole household or team.
  • Copies in our providers' backups are overwritten within a limited time.
